About TRM Solutions

Built on a simple premise: serious people support shouldn't require an enterprise budget.

Keneilwe Mahuma

Founder, MSc (Cand.) Organisational Psychology

Keneilwe has spent over seven years in HR governance, EMEA statutory compliance, and organisational psychology. She started TRM Solutions after watching too many growing businesses choose between an in-house hire they couldn't yet justify, and a retainer that billed them for capacity they didn't need.

TRM Solutions is her answer to that gap: compliance and culture work, delivered à la carte, from one practitioner who does the work herself.

Vision

To be the trusted people strategy partner for growing businesses, making world-class HR governance and organisational psychology accessible to companies of every size.

Mission

Modular, à la carte HR compliance and organisational psychology, built around each client's actual stage. The business sits at the centre. We assemble around them, not the reverse.

Why TRM Solutions

À la carte, not fixed-package

Clients select only the modules they need, priced individually, never bundled into a retainer they didn't ask for.

Compliance and culture, together

Most consultancies specialise in one or the other. TRM Solutions brings statutory compliance and organisational psychology together, backed by an MSc.

Technology-enabled delivery

HRIS administration, process automation, and AI-assisted compliance monitoring keep delivery fast and current, not paper-based and reactive.

Boutique, not bulk

A direct relationship with the practitioner doing the work, not a rotating account team.
